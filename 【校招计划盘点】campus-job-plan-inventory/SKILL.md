# 校招计划初始化与刷新

Use only in a job-search workspace with a job-search profile (`产品管理/求职配置.md`), before `campus-job-application` opens or filters a company's roles. Read the workbook schema in `../【校招岗位收集与投递】campus-job-application/references/workbook-schema.md` before editing.

## 转正实习与覆盖证据

读取 [偏好与证据契约](../【校招岗位收集与投递】campus-job-application/references/preference-contract.md)。按私有配置处理转正实习；conversion_last 在同一汇报排末尾，有正式岗也展示，不需要先完成全部正式覆盖。正式覆盖不足仍须记录缺口，不宣称公司无正式岗。记录 formal_coverage、formal_coverage_refs、suitable_formal_found 和 no_suitable_formal_ref；不把单计划零结果当公司结论，偏好变更不重启首轮。

## One-time project initialization

1. Read the company list from both regional sheets. Create one coverage entry for every distinct region–company identity in `校招计划` before claiming initialization coverage. A blank `计划` with legacy `盘点状态=未盘点` or current `尚未推进` and no plan/role research for the target cohort/current cycle in the Plan or research log is a first-pass queue item, not a discovered plan. Prior-cycle research and a saved company master URL alone do not count as current-cycle plan/role research. A `部分盘点` or `访问受阻` status whose cycle or evidence is unclear is conservatively treated as already visited and deferred for user-selected follow-up; do not reopen it just to resolve its status. If the workbook status conflicts with dated current-cycle Plan/research evidence, use the evidence and correct the status during a user-authorized update.
2. Check the current official campus entry only for companies classified as first-pass-unvisited for the target graduating cohort/current recruiting cycle. Prior-cycle research is a source clue, not current-cycle coverage. A company may have multiple plans, business-unit programs, graduate tracks, and dual-eligible internship programs. Record only what the current source supports; do not treat a historical program as current. Existing current-cycle plan or role research counts as prior coverage even when it is partial; preserve it for the follow-up list.
3. For a first-pass-unvisited company, replace its blank-plan coverage entry with one row per verified plan. Write exactly one of the five agreed values below in `校招计划.盘点状态`; keep company/plan relationships, user stop decisions, closed application windows, eligibility, alias mergers, evidence gaps and source details in the regional `备注` plus the research/Plan record, not as extra status labels. A blank `计划` remains the coverage row if no current plan is verified.

### Five fixed company/plan result values

- `已有相关岗位记录`: at least one verified role matching the active user Query has been written to the regional role sheet with its source. A role title alone or a role belonging only to another cohort is insufficient.
- `无当年校招计划`: accessible official recruitment sources checked for the target cohort show no applicable current-year campus plan. The `备注` names the checked source, cohort and date; this is an observed result, not a claim that the company will never recruit. An older-cycle plan or ordinary social-recruitment page is insufficient current-year evidence.
- `无用户要求岗位`: an applicable current-year campus plan and its relevant accessible role listings/JDs were screened against the active Query, with no qualifying role. The `备注` names the plan, pages/filters checked and exclusion reason. Do not use this value if a relevant page/JD remains unread or only one keyword search returned zero.
- `无法访问校招页面`: an identified applicable campus page or job system could not be read after an actual access attempt. The `备注` gives the exact URL, observed error and unverified scope. This result does not imply either of the two negative hiring outcomes.
- `尚未推进`: no evidence-supported conclusion among the four values above has been reached for this company/plan scope. Historical rows may include partially checked scopes; the current workbook notes and visit evidence must distinguish them. For reporting, a documented visit must be explicitly shown as “首轮已查｜核验未闭环” with the checked scope and gaps, never reported as completely untouched. `尚未推进` alone is never permission to re-search a previously visited scope.

For a company with multiple plans, classify each plan row by its own evidence, then summarize the company from all rows and the regional role sheet. Do not turn a single plan's negative result into a company-wide negative result. Company aliases or business units sharing an already checked source remain mapped in `备注`; “合并”“用户叫停”“已截止”“资格不符”“仅实习无转正” are not sixth or seventh values. Historical free-text statuses are legacy evidence: preserve them until reviewed, and never bulk relabel by keyword.

4. During the first-pass sweep, record any plan relationship that is directly disclosed by the current source. If it is not disclosed, mark the relationship `待核实` and add it to the follow-up list; do not delay the first-pass company queue to investigate every plan pair. After the sweep, research only the plan relationships the user selects. Keep within-plan application limits in `计划内投递限制`, separate from cross-plan relationships.
5. Preserve source URL and date. The following four audit buckets are internal coverage measures, not additional `盘点状态` values. Report mutually exclusive counts for first-pass-covered, first-pass-visited-but-unresolved (a documented current-cycle attempt whose evidence or access remains incomplete), cycle/evidence-ambiguous-deferred (no reliable evidence that the old partial/blocked record belongs to this cycle), and first-pass-unvisited. Report unavailable pages and relationship-unknown items as separate cross-cutting issue counts, not additional company buckets. A queue of unvisited companies is not a completed research pass. Resume the first-pass sweep in batches without reopening companies already attempted, including those with zero results, no current plan found, inaccessible pages, or partial coverage. Keep unresolved relationships in a follow-up list; revisit them only when the user selects the exact company and gap after the first pass.

## Company lookup and refresh

Use this Skill in two ordered stages. **Stage 1 first-pass sweep:** for each in-scope region–company identity with no prior scoped research for the target graduating cohort/current recruiting cycle, reuse the saved `校招主链接` and existing workbook/Plan/research evidence. Research from an older cohort is source context but not current-cycle coverage. Once any actual screen for this cycle is documented, that company/scope has a prior visit for Stage 1 even if the earlier Query was narrower; a later Query change creates a possible follow-up gap and does not restart the sweep. Match coverage by the official ATS tenant, plan ID, filters, result pages and role IDs as well as the displayed company name. If an alias or business-unit row is already covered by the same source scope, record the mapping and reuse its evidence without reopening the portal. If it has a distinct applicable plan/source scope, screen only that uncovered scope. Record every newly verified in-scope role through `campus-job-application`, along with exact unresolved pages and relationships. A documented current-cycle attempt is `first-pass-visited` even when no matching role was found, no current plan appeared, access failed, coverage was partial, or a relationship is unknown; unresolved evidence remains `first-pass-visited-but-unresolved` until complete. If an existing `部分盘点` or `访问受阻` status has unclear cycle/evidence, classify it separately as `cycle/evidence-ambiguous-deferred`; it is neither verified coverage nor a verified current-cycle visit, and do not reopen it to reconstruct history. Do not reopen a visited or deferred scope during Stage 1 because an item remains incomplete or the regional role cells are blank. **Stage 2 targeted follow-up:** after Stage 1, report covered, visited-but-unresolved, ambiguous-deferred and still-unvisited counts plus the exact gaps, then wait for the user to select the exact companies/gaps. A generic `继续` does not authorize Stage 2. Reuse existing pages, filters, results and roles; refresh only evidence that is missing, stale, changed, or specifically selected. Aggregate every `校招计划` row by region–company and source scope before queueing: one documented visit in any row excludes that already checked scope even if another row still says legacy `未盘点` or current `尚未推进` and the regional role cells are blank. A blank-plan legacy `未盘点` or current `尚未推进` row with no prior company/plan research for the current cycle is an unvisited queue item; a populated current-cycle plan record or prior research outcome, including `部分盘点`, is not permission to repeat the company pass. A company master URL alone does not prove a plan pass. `校招计划` rows alone do not prove role coverage, but incomplete role coverage also does not reorder the first-pass queue. Only conclude “no matching role found” after all relevant accessible plans and in-scope results for that company have been checked; name every page or plan that remained inaccessible or unsearched.

### Internship-to-full-time conversion candidates

- When the active Query selects conversion_last, and the official campus entry or a relevant target-family result surfaces internships, flag only the relevant target-family internships for detail-level conversion checks; do not expand to unrelated internship families.
- Inspect the official JD for explicit conversion/retention wording (`实习转正`, `留用`, `转正机会`), target cohort, minimum duration/attendance and performance or other conditions. Hand off a candidate only when the target-family JD is in-scope and the official page explicitly states a conversion opportunity. The role remains an internship, is separately labeled `实习转正候选` in the role-table `备注`, and is never counted as a formal/full-time role or guaranteed conversion.
- Ordinary internships without explicit conversion evidence remain out of scope. Ambiguous or inaccessible wording is recorded as unresolved/pending, not inferred. Track internship plan identity, cross-plan application relationship and limits separately; unknown remains unknown.

Use the parent Skill's [Chrome window grouping](../【校招岗位收集与投递】campus-job-application/references/chrome-window-grouping.md): at most 10 webpage tabs in an Agent-owned window, then create a new window for page 11. Companies may share a window; label every tab by company and never repurpose another company's tab or user window. Keep useful pages while active. At completion, apply the parent's **Close a completed company window** gate to the current company's tabs only; preserve shared-window neighbours, protected and user-owned pages. Inventory does not authorize application forms, uploads, filling or submission.

## Evidence and safety

- Prefer official pages, official APIs and employer announcements. A university-hosted employer announcement may corroborate a relationship, but record its exact URL and wording. Third-party summaries are leads, not sole proof of a quota or plan conflict.
- Do not infer independence from separate apply buttons or separate plan names. Do not infer conflict from a shared login or one account.
- Recheck time-sensitive rules and the logged-in account's remaining opportunities immediately before filling or submitting under the main Skill.
- Use the Spreadsheets Skill for every workbook edit, with a recoverable backup before structural changes and saved-file/visual validation afterward.

查询、续跑和汇报前必须按[研究进度强制核对](../【校招岗位收集与投递】campus-job-application/references/research-progress.md)读取当前工作簿状态、备注和有效流水，运行覆盖记录校验。历史“首轮已查”状态/备注是访问证据，不能被旧标签枚举忽略。
