# 校招计划初始化与刷新

Use only in a job-search workspace with a job-search profile (`产品管理/求职配置.md`), before `campus-job-application` opens or filters a company's roles. Read the workbook schema in `../【校招岗位收集与投递】campus-job-application/references/workbook-schema.md` before editing.

## One-time project initialization

1. Read the company list from both regional sheets. Create one coverage entry for every distinct region–company identity in `校招计划` before claiming initialization coverage. A blank `计划` with `盘点状态=未盘点` and no plan/role research for the target cohort/current cycle in the Plan or research log is a first-pass queue item, not a discovered plan. Prior-cycle research and a saved company master URL alone do not count as current-cycle plan/role research. A `部分盘点` or `访问受阻` status whose cycle or evidence is unclear is conservatively treated as already visited and deferred for user-selected follow-up; do not reopen it just to resolve its status. If the workbook status conflicts with dated current-cycle Plan/research evidence, use the evidence and correct the status during a user-authorized update.
2. Check the current official campus entry only for companies classified as first-pass-unvisited for the target graduating cohort/current recruiting cycle. Prior-cycle research is a source clue, not current-cycle coverage. A company may have multiple plans, business-unit programs, graduate tracks, and dual-eligible internship programs. Record only what the current source supports; do not treat a historical program as current. Existing current-cycle plan or role research counts as prior coverage even when it is partial; preserve it for the follow-up list.
3. For a first-pass-unvisited company, replace its blank-plan coverage entry with one row per verified plan. Retain a company-level coverage row if no plan can be verified, with `计划` blank and an explicit `盘点状态` such as `首轮已查｜访问受阻` or `首轮已查｜未发现本届计划`. A zero matching-role result is also a first-pass outcome: mark `首轮已查｜无匹配岗位` only after the accessible applicable plans and results have been checked. Put a concise outcome and cohort in the regional company placeholder row's `备注` when no role row is added. Never turn a failed search into proof that the company has no plan.
4. During the first-pass sweep, record any plan relationship that is directly disclosed by the current source. If it is not disclosed, mark the relationship `待核实` and add it to the follow-up list; do not delay the first-pass company queue to investigate every plan pair. After the sweep, research only the plan relationships the user selects. Keep within-plan application limits in `计划内投递限制`, separate from cross-plan relationships.
5. Preserve source URL and date. Report mutually exclusive counts for first-pass-covered, first-pass-visited-but-unresolved (a documented current-cycle attempt whose evidence or access remains incomplete), cycle/evidence-ambiguous-deferred (no reliable evidence that the old partial/blocked record belongs to this cycle), and first-pass-unvisited. Report unavailable pages and relationship-unknown items as separate cross-cutting issue counts, not additional company buckets. A queue of unvisited companies is not a completed research pass. Resume the first-pass sweep in batches without reopening companies already attempted, including those with zero results, no current plan found, inaccessible pages, or partial coverage. Keep unresolved relationships in a follow-up list; revisit them only when the user selects the exact company and gap after the first pass.

## Company lookup and refresh

Use this Skill in two ordered stages. **Stage 1 first-pass sweep:** for each in-scope region–company identity with no prior scoped research for the target graduating cohort/current recruiting cycle, reuse the saved `校招主链接` and existing workbook/Plan/research evidence. Research from an older cohort is source context but not current-cycle coverage. Once any actual screen for this cycle is documented, that company/scope has a prior visit for Stage 1 even if the earlier Query was narrower; a later Query change creates a possible follow-up gap and does not restart the sweep. Match coverage by the official ATS tenant, plan ID, filters, result pages and role IDs as well as the displayed company name. If an alias or business-unit row is already covered by the same source scope, record the mapping and reuse its evidence without reopening the portal. If it has a distinct applicable plan/source scope, screen only that uncovered scope. Record every newly verified in-scope role through `campus-job-application`, along with exact unresolved pages and relationships. A documented current-cycle attempt is `first-pass-visited` even when no matching role was found, no current plan appeared, access failed, coverage was partial, or a relationship is unknown; unresolved evidence remains `first-pass-visited-but-unresolved` until complete. If an existing `部分盘点` or `访问受阻` status has unclear cycle/evidence, classify it separately as `cycle/evidence-ambiguous-deferred`; it is neither verified coverage nor a verified current-cycle visit, and do not reopen it to reconstruct history. Do not reopen a visited or deferred scope during Stage 1 because an item remains incomplete or the regional role cells are blank. **Stage 2 targeted follow-up:** after Stage 1, report covered, visited-but-unresolved, ambiguous-deferred and still-unvisited counts plus the exact gaps, then wait for the user to select the exact companies/gaps. A generic `继续` does not authorize Stage 2. Reuse existing pages, filters, results and roles; refresh only evidence that is missing, stale, changed, or specifically selected. Aggregate every `校招计划` row by region–company and source scope before queueing: one documented visit in any row excludes that already checked scope even if another row still says `未盘点` and the regional role cells are blank. A blank-plan `未盘点` row with no prior company/plan research for the current cycle is an unvisited queue item; a populated current-cycle plan record or prior research outcome, including `部分盘点`, is not permission to repeat the company pass. A company master URL alone does not prove a plan pass. `校招计划` rows alone do not prove role coverage, but incomplete role coverage also does not reorder the first-pass queue. Only conclude “no matching role found” after all relevant accessible plans and in-scope results for that company have been checked; name every page or plan that remained inaccessible or unsearched.

### Internship-to-full-time conversion candidates

- When the active Query targets formal/full-time campus roles but the official campus entry or a relevant target-family result surfaces internships, flag only the relevant target-family internships for detail-level conversion checks; do not expand to unrelated internship families.
- Inspect the official JD for explicit conversion/retention wording (`实习转正`, `留用`, `转正机会`), target cohort, minimum duration/attendance and performance or other conditions. Hand off a candidate only when the target-family JD is in-scope and the official page explicitly states a conversion opportunity. The role remains an internship, is separately labeled `实习转正候选` in the role-table `备注`, and is never counted as a formal/full-time role or guaranteed conversion.
- Ordinary internships without explicit conversion evidence remain out of scope. Ambiguous or inaccessible wording is recorded as unresolved/pending, not inferred. Track internship plan identity, cross-plan application relationship and limits separately; unknown remains unknown.

Use one dedicated Chrome window per company. Keep that company's master, plan-filtered results and role pages inside it; do not repurpose another company's window. Keep useful pages open while the company investigation remains active. At its end, hand control to `campus-job-application`'s **Close a completed company window** gate: the Query-confirmed `信息收集` mode authorizes closing an agent-created, unprotected company window after recording its outcome; `准备投递` retains it. Protected or user-owned pages remain open. Capturing plan metadata does not authorize opening application forms, uploading a résumé, filling, submitting, or closing user pages.

## Evidence and safety

- Prefer official pages, official APIs and employer announcements. A university-hosted employer announcement may corroborate a relationship, but record its exact URL and wording. Third-party summaries are leads, not sole proof of a quota or plan conflict.
- Do not infer independence from separate apply buttons or separate plan names. Do not infer conflict from a shared login or one account.
- Recheck time-sensitive rules and the logged-in account's remaining opportunities immediately before filling or submitting under the main Skill.
- Use the Spreadsheets Skill for every workbook edit, with a recoverable backup before structural changes and saved-file/visual validation afterward.
